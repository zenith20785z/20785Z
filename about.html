Our journey: 21549B Omega

Three of Zenith's current members previously competed in VEX IQ as team 21549B Omega. Omega took part in the VEX IQ UK National Championship and won the Design Award. The engineering, design and documentation experience from those seasons laid the foundations for Zenith.
